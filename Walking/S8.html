Walk to Ettrick and Yarrow Show

📏 Distance: 14km ⛰️ Difficulty: Easy ⏱️ Est. Time: 3.5 hrs

From Victoria Park we cross Ettrick Water at Selkirk Bridge and follow Ettrickhaugh Road and the Mill Lade to the Waterwheel Café in Philiphaugh Estate. We then walk parallel to the busy A708 leading to Bowhill Estate via the General’s Bridge. Following the B7039 we reach the Ettrick and Yarrow Show site at Carterhaugh. After experiencing the Show, and it’s displays of the finest livestock, produce and tractors, we retrace out steps back along the B7039, turning right at Gilkeekit Cottages and crossing the river by a footbridge near Oakwood Mill Farm. We then head up the hill and across Middlestead Burn, bearing left at Brownmoor (185m) to tak the path to The Haining via Common Burn and Haining Moss before passing the Auld Kirkyard as we return to the town centre.
