Hawick Loop.

📏 Distance: 75km ⛰️ Difficulty: Hard ⏱️ Est. Time: 3 - 5 hrs

Head up the Ettrick valley, through Ettrickbridge and turn East after the Tushie Law inn. Continue climbing up to Alemoor Loch, just a final push to the top now then enjoy stunning views as you decend to Hawick. Lots of refreshment options here, including the Borders Distillery: head back to through the rolling countryside to Selkirk.
